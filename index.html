About the Boutique

A small boutique with a big heart

Zahra Rubab Ladies Boutique sits at Shop 146, Jadoon Plaza Phase 3 — a name Abbottabad’s families already know and trust. We are a ladies-only boutique where mothers, daughters and brides shop together, at ease.

Every piece on our rails is chosen by hand: fabrics we have touched, embroidery we have checked stitch by stitch, and colours that flatter real women. If a suit needs a tuck, a longer sleeve or a matching dupatta, we help you sort it before you leave — that is simply how a neighbourhood boutique should work.
